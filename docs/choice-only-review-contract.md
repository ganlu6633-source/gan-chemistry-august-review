# Choice-only review contract

All junior and senior secondary review uses the existing A–D interaction. A student reads the complete question, works on paper, selects an answer, and receives server-graded feedback with an explanation. Do not introduce written responses, self-ratings, or recall checklists.

Original multiple-choice questions retain their source conditions and options. A source fill-in or constructed-response subquestion may be converted into four choices, with the added distractors and independent explanation identified as an adaptation. Hold questions whose source, conditions, unique answer, formula, image, or explanation has not been verified.

Reserve questions are explicit, reviewed bindings from a particular original option to three to five real questions. A broad skill or chapter tag alone does not justify a binding. Each candidate records its source identity, current revision, and a specific same-knowledge/same-type reason. Different transcriptions of one question and subquestions sharing one parent must not count as independent evidence within a reserve branch.

The first recorded choice determines the branch. A wrong answer starts its eligible reserve questions; errors can extend the branch to five. No unrelated question may fill a shortage. Fewer than three available independent candidates is a reserve gap. Three consecutive correct independent choices can support consolidation; finishing a daily quota or selecting a self-rating cannot establish mastery. Another unresolved error must remain visible.

Preserve first answers and source revisions through retries and resume. A stale or changed source must not overwrite a recorded answer. Teacher previews exercise the same routing without writing student results. An expanded high-school attempt must submit the complete server-issued order. Junior practice keeps its daily cap and persists unfinished branches for later eligible sessions.

Release integrity checks validate the complete question/option/answer/explanation and private image assets. Source-content changes require a staged revision, a reviewable before/after audit, and atomic activation. Keep historical attempts and source records intact. Actual per-option coverage is a separate measure from the size or structural validity of a released bank; report both honestly.
